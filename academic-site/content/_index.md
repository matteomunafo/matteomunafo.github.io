<section class="block featured">
  <h2 class="label">Master's thesis · 2026</h2>
  <p class="featured-title"><a href="/research/strict-univalent-universes/">Strict univalent universes of an ∞-topos are model independent</a></p>
  <p>
    For any ∞-topos, the choice of a strict univalent universe of a fixed size,
    coming from a Cisinski model category that presents it, is essentially unique:
    the groupoid of such universes is a contractible space. The thesis also gives a
    proof of Shulman's construction that avoids simplicial structures.
  </p>
