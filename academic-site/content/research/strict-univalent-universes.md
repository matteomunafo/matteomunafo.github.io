## Abstract

For any ∞-topos, the choice of a strict univalent universe of a fixed cardinality,
coming from a Cisinski model category that presents the ∞-topos, is essentially
unique: the groupoid spanned by such universes is a contractible space. The thesis
also simplifies Shulman's proof that every ∞-topos is presented by a model of
homotopy type theory, avoiding simplicial structures and giving a different
construction of the universe.

## Mathematical context

An **∞-topos** is an important setting for higher category theory and homotopy
theory. One can study logical and type-theoretic constructions in presentations
of an ∞-topos by model categories. In this setting, **univalent universes** play
a central role in interpreting **homotopy type theory**.

A natural question is whether the resulting strict univalent universes depend
on the particular presentation of the ∞-topos. The thesis addresses this
**model-independence question** in the setting of **Cisinski model categories**.

## Main result and construction

For a fixed size, the space of choices of strict univalent universes described
in the thesis is contractible. In this sense the choice is essentially unique,
even when considered through the specified model-category presentations.

The thesis also presents a construction related to Shulman's existence result
for univalent universes, using an approach that avoids simplicial structures.
For precise hypotheses, definitions and proofs, consult the full thesis.
