## Strict univalent universes of an ∞-topos are model independent

*Master's thesis, ALGANT Master in Mathematics (Università degli Studi di Milano and
Universität Regensburg), 2026 — supervisor: Prof. Denis-Charles Cisinski*

**Abstract.** For any ∞-topos, the choice of a strict univalent universe of a fixed
cardinality, coming from a Cisinski model category that presents the ∞-topos, is
essentially unique: the groupoid spanned by such universes is a contractible space.
The thesis also simplifies Shulman's proof that every ∞-topos is presented by a model
of homotopy type theory, avoiding simplicial structures and giving a different
construction of the universe.
