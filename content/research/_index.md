My research is in **higher category theory**, with an eye towards **algebraic geometry**.
At the moment I am interested in the **internal semantics of higher topoi** and in
**homotopy type theory** as a language for reasoning inside them. I approach most
topics from a heavily categorical point of view.

## Univalent universes in ∞-topoi

A 1-topos has an internal logic that behaves like a generalised theory of sets. It is
natural to ask whether ∞-topoi carry a logic in a similar way. Since ∞-categories have
no strict equalities of compositions and identities, one first needs a *strictification*:
a 1-category presenting the ∞-topos, with an honest logic that can be studied.

Shulman proved in 2019 that every ∞-topos is presented by a model category that models
homotopy type theory, the hardest part being the univalence axiom. Left open was whether
this semantics depends on the chosen model. In my master's thesis, written with
Prof. Denis-Charles Cisinski, I show that as long as the model is reasonable (a Cisinski
model category) the strict univalent universes of a fixed size form a **contractible
space of choices**. I also give an alternative construction of Shulman's universe that
avoids simplicial structures.

I recently extended the main result to π-tribes. I'm now working to prove that all π-tribes that localize to the a fixed topos and model HoTT form a contractible space.
