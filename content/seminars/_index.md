## Stable and symmetric monoidal categories for derived algebraic geometry

*Oberseminar "Higher Zariski Geometry", Universität Regensburg*

Stable ∞-categories (pointed categories, equivalent definitions of stability,
additivity, the triangulated structure on the homotopy category, examples) and symmetric
monoidal ∞-categories with their commutative algebras and modules, ring spectra and
their modules. Mainly from Cnossen's notes on stable homotopy theory and higher algebra,
and the paper *Higher Zariski Geometry* by Aoki, Barthel, Chedalavada, Schlank and
Stevenson.

[Notes (PDF)](/seminars/higher-zariski-geometry.pdf)

## Applications of the triangulation theorem for o-minimal structures

*Course "O-minimal structures"*

The Euler characteristic determines the definable isomorphism class of a definable set;
definable homotopies and extension properties of definable maps into contractible
spaces. Following van den Dries, *Tame topology and o-minimal structures*.

[Notes (PDF)](/seminars/o-minimal-triangulation.pdf)

## Finite group schemes *(in Italian)*

*Bachelor in Mathematics, Università degli Studi di Milano*

A study of parts of R. Pink's notes *Finite group schemes*, aiming at the theorem that
finite commutative group schemes over a field of characteristic zero are étale.
Written under the guidance of Prof. Fabrizio Andreatta.

[Notes (PDF)](/seminars/finite-group-schemes.pdf)
